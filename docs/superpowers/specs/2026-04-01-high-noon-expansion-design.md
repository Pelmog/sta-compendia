# High Noon Expansion — Posse, Surprises, and Third-Party Chaos

**Date:** 2026-04-01
**Parent Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-03-30-high-noon-encounter-design.md`
**Adventure:** Pale Riders of the Lost Frontier, Session 2
**Foundry Scene:** "Battlemap — Yara's Ranch (High Noon)" (`kUynRZA8CNuYNpYF`)

---

## Overview

Expands the High Noon encounter with four new systems layered on top of the existing design:

1. **Town Posse** — the crew can rally help from Voss Landing during morning prep, and separately the town may ride in if things go badly
2. **Dalton Surprises** — things the Daltons have prepared that the crew doesn't expect
3. **Environmental Wildcards** — subspace anomalies and sandstorm
4. **Third-Party Chaos** — Taris exploits the fight, and the bunker stirs below

### Escalating Stakes

The encounter self-adjusts. A well-prepared crew gets a satisfying western shootout. A crew that didn't prepare gets a desperate siege. The worse it gets, the more likely Taris intervenes — and the higher the price of her help.

- **Tense but heroic** (default): Farmhouse can catch fire, situation feels desperate, but escape route through tunnel remains viable. Classic TOS.
- **Genuinely threatening** (triggered by neglect): If the crew ignores the farmhouse or lets Sev take command without response, Tomás could be injured, Yara captured, the tunnel blocked. Nobody dies, but injuries and captures are on the table.

## 1. The Town Posse

Two independent paths to reinforcements: the crew rides to town (active), or the town rides to the crew (reactive).

### Path A: Rally the Town (Morning Preparation)

If a PC goes back to Voss Landing during the morning prep phase to ask for help, they find a town paralysed by fear. Nobody wants to cross Dalton. Rallying help requires making a case to specific NPCs — each has their own reason to say yes or no.

**Base roll:** Presence + Command, Difficulty 3

**Modifiers based on Session 1 relationships:**

| Modifier | Condition |
|----------|-----------|
| -1 Diff | Crew genuinely connected with Marta (not just bought drinks — helped, listened, showed respect) |
| -1 Diff | Healer bonded with Doc Lenn (the tricorder scene, medical respect) |
| -1 Diff | Any PC defended Yara publicly when she rode into town |
| +1 Diff | Crew was secretive or dismissive with the townsfolk |
| +1 Diff | Crew didn't intervene when Dalton confronted them |

If the roll succeeds, the GM picks from the available posse members based on which NPCs the crew built relationships with.

### Town Posse Members

Each NPC who joins gets a character beat — a moment the GM can play during the ride out, the preparation, or the fight itself.

#### Marta Saal — "The Mother"

**Joins if:** The crew connected with her in Session 1. She's doing this for Tomás and Yara, not for strangers.

**Weapons:** Verathi Shotgun (borrowed from under the bar)

**Character Beat — The Ride Out:**
> Marta rides a runner hard, shotgun across the saddle. She hasn't fired a weapon in ten years. When someone comments on this, she says: *"I fired one plenty before Tomás was born. Turns out having a child doesn't make you forget. Just makes you angrier."*

**Character Beat — During the Fight:**
> If Tomás is in danger (farmhouse breached, fire), Marta abandons whatever position she's holding and moves toward him. She doesn't ask permission. She doesn't coordinate. She goes. If a PC tries to stop her: *"That's my son in there. You can help or you can move."*

**Combat Role:** Fights from the corral zone or water tanks. Not tactically disciplined — she shoots at whoever's closest to the farmhouse. Shotgun is devastating at close range.

#### Doc Lenn — "The Healer"

**Joins if:** Healer bonded with him in Session 1. He's not a fighter — he's coming because people will get hurt and someone needs to patch them up.

**Weapons:** None. Carries his medical bag.

**Character Beat — The Ride Out:**
> Doc Lenn rides slowly. His hands shake on the reins. When someone asks if he's alright: *"I'm seventy-three years old, I haven't ridden a runner in fifteen years, and I'm heading toward gunfire. I'm having the time of my life."*

**Character Beat — During the Fight:**
> Doc Lenn sets up in the farmhouse as a field medic. Any PC or ally who takes an Injury can have Doc Lenn treat it as a Task action (Insight + Medicine, Diff 2) — on success, the Injury is stabilised (still present but no longer worsening). He works with Yara's kitchen table as an operating surface.

**Character Beat — The Bunker Tunnel:**
> If the crew retreats to or discovers the bunker tunnel, Doc Lenn goes quiet. He stares at the tunnel entrance. He hasn't been underground in forty years. His sister is down there. He says nothing. But if anyone asks: *"That's the way I came out. I was eleven. The door closed behind me and I thought it would never open again."*

**Combat Role:** Non-combatant. Stays in the farmhouse. Provides medical support (one heal action per round). If the farmhouse is breached, he shields Tomás with his body.

#### Tomás's Runner Gang — "The Kids"

**Joins if:** Any PC connected with Tomás. He didn't ask permission — he rounded up three of his friends (ages 13-16) on runners.

**Weapons:** Slingshots and rocks. They're children.

**They do NOT fight.** What they do:

**Character Beat — Arrival:**
> Tomás rides up with three other kids on runners. Marta sees them and goes white. *"Tomás Saal, what in the twin hells—"* Tomás: *"We're not fighting. We're scouting."*

**Tactical Value:**
- **Scouts:** The kids know every canyon path, gully, and dry riverbed within 10km. If they're sent to scout before noon, the crew automatically discovers Dalton's approach route (Prep #8 — crew acts first in Round 1). No roll needed.
- **Runner relay:** If the farmhouse is surrounded or communications cut, Tomás can ride a message back to town on a runner. Fast.
- **Distraction:** The kids can ride runners through the approach road kicking up dust — creates the "Dust devil" effect (+1 Diff ranged attacks) without needing a table roll. One use — Marta orders them out after.

**Character Beat — If things go badly:**
> If the farmhouse catches fire or is breached, Tomás doesn't flee. He grabs the data crystal from his pocket and runs toward the tunnel entrance. *"I know where to go. I always know where to go."* This can trigger the Bunker Emergence event (see Section 4).

### Path C: The Town Rides In (Reactive)

Independent of Path A. If the crew didn't rally help, the town might still come — but later and less effectively.

**Trigger conditions (GM checks at end of Round 3, and every round after):**

The townsfolk are watching from the canyon rim road (word travels fast in a small town). They ride in when ALL of these are true:
- At least one squad is still at full or half stress (the fight is real)
- The farmhouse is threatened (fire, breach, or Dalton forces in the farmhouse zone)
- The crew has demonstrated they'll stand and fight (haven't fled)

**Who comes:**

| Trust Level | Who Rides In | Effect |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Crew connected with 3+ NPCs in Session 1** | Marta + 3 armed townsfolk | Full squad equivalent (Notable, Stress 8). Arrives Round 4+. |
| **Crew connected with 1-2 NPCs** | 2 armed townsfolk | Minor NPCs. Arrive Round 5+. Provide covering fire only. |
| **Crew was distant/secretive** | Nobody | The town watches. If the crew loses, Marta takes Tomás and leaves. |

**Character Beat — Arrival:**
> The sound of runners. Dust cloud from the canyon road. Marta's voice: *"Voss Landing stands!"* — a line that means nothing historically but everything in this moment.

### New Actor to Create

| Actor | NPC Type | Stress | Weapons | Traits |
|-------|----------|--------|---------|--------|
| **Voss Landing Townsfolk** | Notable | 8 | Verathi Six-Gun, Verathi Long Rifle | Verathi, Townsfolk, Fighting for Home |

Uses the `dalton-rider-alt.png` portrait (the tough rider image — works for a determined townsfolk fighter too).

## 2. Dalton Surprises

Things the Daltons have prepared that the crew doesn't expect. These are GM tools — use them when the fight needs a complication or when the crew is doing too well.

### The Powder Wagon

Sev brought a wagon loaded with mining charges along the approach road. It's pulled by two runners and driven by a hired hand.

**When it appears:** Round 2 or later. Sev orders it forward when the squads stall on the approach road. It appears at the edge of the approach road zone.

**What it does:**
- The wagon is heading for the farmhouse. If it reaches the farmhouse zone (2 rounds after appearing), the charges detonate — **6 damage, Area, to everyone in the farmhouse zone**. The farmhouse catches fire automatically. Yara's barricade (Prep #7) is destroyed.
- The crew can stop it:
  - **Shoot the runners** (Daring + Security, Diff 1) — the runners bolt sideways, wagon veers off. Detonates harmlessly in open desert.
  - **Shoot the driver** (Control + Security, Diff 2) — driver drops, wagon rolls to a stop. Charges can be salvaged (one-use Mining Charge for the crew).
  - **Shoot the charges** (Control + Security, Diff 3) — the wagon detonates immediately. 5 damage, Area, to everyone in the approach road zone. Spectacular but dangerous.
  - **Stampede runners into it** (if Prep #3 active) — runners collide with wagon, same effect as shooting the runners.

**Character Beat:**
> Rek Dalton laughs when the wagon appears. *"Sev's idea. Boy always was the clever one."* If Rek is already down, Sev doesn't announce it — the wagon just appears.

### Sev's Canyon Mines

Sev has mined the canyon path between the ranch and town. Not to kill — to cut off retreat and reinforcements.

**When it matters:** If the crew tries to flee toward town, or if the posse rides in via the canyon road.

**Effect:** Anyone moving through the canyon path must roll Insight + Security, Diff 1 (or Daring + Conn if riding a runner). Failure: 3 damage + prone + Complication "Path Blocked." Success: they spot the tripwire and bypass it.

**Discovery:** If the crew scouted Dalton's approach route (Prep #8), they also discover the canyon mines. Thon or Malevolant: *"They've mined the back trail. Nobody's getting in or out without knowing about these."* The mines can be disarmed (Reason + Engineering, Diff 2) or the crew can warn the posse about them.

**If the posse arrives without warning:** Roll for the lead rider. On failure, the lead runner goes down — Complication "Rider Down." Not lethal, but the posse is delayed one round while they regroup.

### Dalton's Ultimatum

If both squads are broken but Sev is still alive and in command, Sev doesn't retreat. He escalates.

**Trigger:** Both Squad A and Squad B are at 0 stress. Sev still standing. Farmhouse not yet breached.

**Event:** Sev sends Pip (if Pip is still on the field and hasn't been talked down) forward with a white flag. Pip is terrified. He delivers Sev's message:

> *"Your friends in the house have ten minutes. Walk them out, walk away, and nobody else gets hurt. Stay, and I'll burn it down around them."*

This is not a bluff. Sev will set the farmhouse on fire (incendiary shots from the canyon rim) if the crew doesn't respond. But it's also an opening — T'Karra can negotiate, or the crew can use the ten minutes to evacuate through the tunnel.

**If Pip has already been talked down:** Sev delivers the message himself, walking into the open. This is the most dangerous version — Sev exposed, but radiating calm lethality. A PC could take the shot. Or talk.

## 3. Environmental Wildcards

### Subspace Anomaly Events

The dimensional scars near Verath III occasionally pulse. These are unpredictable, affect technology, and remind everyone that this planet is wounded.

**Add to the Chaos of Battle table as a Threat spend (2 Threat) or replace rerolled results:**

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Subspace Flicker** | All energy weapons (phasers, if active) shut down for 1 round. Verathi chemical weapons unaffected. Tricorders display garbage for 30 seconds. Any active communicator channels fill with static — the crew is cut off from the Enterprise for this round. |
| **Gravity Ripple** | A wave of altered gravity crosses the battlefield from east to west. Everyone in the open (not behind cover) rolls Fitness + Security Diff 1 or is knocked prone. Loose objects (ammunition, tools, the powder wagon if it's moving) shift 1 zone east. Runners panic. |
| **Temporal Echo** | For a heartbeat, everyone on the battlefield sees a ghost image — the ranch as it was 300 years ago, intact, green fields, the bunker entrance a proper doorway with people walking through it. Then it's gone. No mechanical effect, but it's a reminder: this place had a life before Dalton. Healer senses a wave of old grief — thousands of people, underground, afraid. |
| **Dimensional Scar Pulse** | A visible crack in the air — shimmering, prismatic, running from the canyon rim to the water tanks. Anything that passes through it takes 1 damage (subspace friction). It persists for 2 rounds, then fades. Effectively creates a wall across the battlefield that costs health to cross. |

### The Sandstorm

Not a random event — the GM deploys this when the pacing needs a reset. Mid-fight, a wall of dust rolls in from the desert.

**Trigger:** GM decision. Best used when:
- The fight has been going 4+ rounds and needs a gear shift
- The crew needs a breather to regroup
- Sev has just taken command and the GM wants a dramatic transition between phases

**Mechanical Effect (lasts 2 rounds):**
- **Visibility:** +2 Difficulty to all ranged attacks beyond the current zone
- **Movement:** +1 Difficulty to all movement between zones (except under cover)
- **Cover:** Everyone counts as having partial cover (the dust itself)
- **Opportunity:** Characters can reposition unseen. Sev uses this — when the storm clears, he's somewhere new and dangerous

**Character Beat:**
> The twin suns dim. A wall of ochre dust, three storeys tall, rolls across the approach road. Visibility drops to arm's length. The gunfire stops. For thirty seconds, the only sound is wind and the groaning of the water tanks.

**What happens during the storm:**
- Dalton forces reposition (GM moves tokens to new tactical positions)
- If the posse is en route, they arrive during the storm — their first volley comes out of the dust
- Taris uses the storm for cover (see Section 4)
- The farmhouse door blows open if not barricaded — dust fills the interior

## 4. Third-Party Chaos

### Taris's Gambit

Commander Taris has been waiting for exactly this. She knew about the fight — Malevolant might have caught her soldiers pulling back from the area. The High Noon confrontation is an opportunity she's been engineering from the sidelines.

**Taris's Goals (in priority order):**
1. Access to the bunker tunnel under Yara's ranch
2. The warp proof (in the bunkers or the Science Academy)
3. Keep the crew alive (she needs them to deal with the Warden — she can't)
4. Maintain plausible deniability with Starfleet

**Taris operates on two tracks simultaneously:**

#### Track 1: The Ranch (Taris herself)

**When she appears:** Round 4 or later, whenever is most dramatic. During the sandstorm is ideal.

**How she appears:** Taris walks out of the dust. No weapon drawn. Her two Romulan soldiers are behind her, weapons holstered. She's smiling.

**What she does depends on the battle state:**

| Battle State | Taris's Action |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Crew is winning** | She approaches whoever is coordinating the defence. *"Impressive. You fight well for scientists. I notice you haven't used those very interesting weapons yet."* She's probing — do they have phasers? She offers nothing. She watches. She's looking for the tunnel entrance. |
| **Fight is even** | She offers help: *"I have two soldiers. Very good soldiers. They could end this in ninety seconds."* The price: she wants to see what's in Yara's cellar. *"I know there's a door down there. I've known for a week. I just can't open it without making a mess."* |
| **Crew is losing** | She intervenes without asking. Her soldiers fire disruptor pistols (the first energy weapons on the battlefield) and drop two targets instantly. Then she turns to the crew: *"You owe me. And I think we both know what's underground."* The price is non-negotiable: she goes into the tunnel with the crew. |

**Character Beat — If the crew refuses her help:**
> Taris shrugs. *"Preferences are not promises."* She withdraws to the canyon rim and watches. Her soldiers cover her. She doesn't help the Daltons either — she's not that petty. But she takes note of who goes into the cellar.

**Character Beat — If the crew accepts:**
> Taris's soldiers are terrifyingly efficient. Disruptor fire, precise, controlled. The Dalton riders have never seen energy weapons. Morale breaks instantly. But now Taris is inside the farmhouse, standing next to the tunnel entrance, and she's not leaving.

#### Track 2: The Science Academy (Centurion Reva)

**Runs simultaneously with Track 1, off-screen.**

While Taris is at the ranch, she's sent Centurion Reva and two soldiers to the Science Academy ruins to secure whatever warp proof materials are there.

**The crew doesn't know this is happening** unless:
- Malevolant tracks Taris's movements and notices the split force (Insight + Security, Diff 2 — only if Malevolant is specifically watching Taris)
- Vex left monitoring equipment at the Science Academy in Session 1 (improvise based on what happened)
- The Enterprise detects disruptor fire at the Academy (if the crew is in contact with the ship)

**Consequence:** If nobody intercepts Reva, the Romulans secure half the warp proof by the time the crew enters the bunkers. This raises the stakes for Session 3 — the Romulans have leverage.

**If the crew splits to intercept:** This stretches the crew thin. Whoever goes to the Academy misses the ranch fight. Whoever stays at the ranch faces Dalton without their full team. Exactly what Taris wants.

### The Bunker Emergence

The bunker tunnel in Yara's cellar connects to the Warden's underground city. The Warden is aware of surface activity — it monitors seismic and acoustic data. The High Noon fight registers.

**Trigger:** Any of the following:
- Tomás runs to the tunnel with his data crystal (when the farmhouse is threatened)
- The crew retreats into the cellar
- The powder wagon detonation (seismic event)
- The pre-war ordnance detonation (seismic event)

**What happens:**

#### Stage 1: The Tunnel Stirs

The tunnel entrance in Yara's cellar hums. Lights flicker on — ancient, amber, recessed into the walls. A draft of cold, filtered air rises from below. The air smells of recycled atmosphere and something faintly chemical.

> **The Warden's voice** (if the crew is near the entrance): *"Surface contaminants detected. Atmospheric filtration engaged. Citizens are advised to remain in designated safety zones."*

The voice is calm, warm, parental. It's speaking to the bunker dwellers, not the crew. But the crew can hear it.

**Mechanical effect:** The tunnel is open. Anyone can enter. Going underground ends their participation in the surface fight (they're in a different encounter space).

#### Stage 2: Tomás and the Crystal

If Tomás reaches the tunnel with his data crystal, something unexpected happens.

The crystal reacts to the tunnel's proximity — it glows, faintly, pulsing at 72 BPM (the carrier frequency of the original signal). The tunnel lights pulse in sync.

> Tomás stares at the crystal. *"It's never done that before."*

**What this means:** The data crystal is a key. Sera Voss designed the broadcast signal to be both a message to the stars AND an authentication token for the Warden's systems. The crystal doesn't open the bunker — but it identifies its holder as "authorised personnel." The Warden won't immediately lock the doors on anyone carrying it.

**Mechanical effect:** Anyone carrying the crystal when entering the bunker gets one free pass — the Warden doesn't deploy sedation gas or combat drones for the first 10 minutes. After that, the Warden adapts.

This is a **Session 2 → Session 3 bridge**: the crystal buys the crew time to enter the bunker safely, setting up the Warden encounter.

#### Stage 3: The Last Stand Below

If the crew is forced into the cellar by fire, breach, or overwhelming force, the encounter shifts from "defend the ranch" to "hold the tunnel entrance."

**New zone: The Cellar**
- Heavy cover (stone walls, narrow entrance)
- Only 2 people can fight from the entrance at once (chokepoint)
- Attackers must come through the farmhouse above (if it's on fire, they take fire damage too)
- Yara knows the cellar layout — she gets +1d20 to all attacks from this position

**Character Beat — Yara's decision:**
> If the crew retreats to the cellar, Yara doesn't follow immediately. She stands at the top of the stairs, shotgun pointed at the door. *"My grandmother built this tunnel. My mother kept it secret. I'm not letting Rek Dalton walk down these stairs."* She fires once, then descends and bars the cellar door.

**Character Beat — Doc Lenn at the threshold:**
> If Doc Lenn is present and the tunnel is open, he freezes at the threshold. Forty years. The amber lights. The recycled air. He can't move. Someone has to talk him through it or take his hand.

**Endgame:** The cellar is holdable. The crew can wait out the fire, the Daltons can't breach the stone walls, and eventually Sev either retreats or the posse arrives. But if Taris is in the farmhouse, she follows them down — and now the Session 3 bunker encounter begins with a Romulan commander at their shoulder.

## 5. New Roll Table: Battlefield Surprises (d8)

A supplementary table the GM can roll on when they want to inject something unexpected. Not rolled automatically — this is a GM tool for when the fight needs energy.

| d8 | Event | Effect |
|----|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Powder wagon appears** | Sev's wagon enters the approach road. 2 rounds to reach farmhouse. See Section 2. |
| 2 | **Canyon mines triggered** | Someone on the canyon path hits a tripwire. 3 damage + prone + path blocked. |
| 3 | **Subspace flicker** | Energy weapons offline 1 round. Communicators static. Tricorders garbage. |
| 4 | **Gravity ripple** | Everyone in the open: Fitness + Security Diff 1 or prone. Loose objects shift 1 zone. |
| 5 | **Sandstorm incoming** | Wall of dust. +2 Diff ranged beyond current zone. Lasts 2 rounds. Everyone repositions. |
| 6 | **Temporal echo** | Ghost image of the ranch 300 years ago. No mechanical effect. Healer senses grief from below. |
| 7 | **Taris appears** | Commander Taris walks out of the dust/canyon/chaos. See Section 4 for behaviour by battle state. |
| 8 | **The tunnel stirs** | Lights in Yara's cellar flicker on. Cold air rises. The Warden's voice echoes. Something is waking up below. |

## 8. What This Design Does NOT Include

- No stat blocks for Taris's soldiers beyond the new "Romulan Soldier" actor — they're deliberately overwhelming (the point is that Taris's help comes at a cost, not that her soldiers are beatable)
- No map for the Science Academy raid (that's off-screen — the consequence matters, not the fight)
- No bunker interior map (that's the Session 3 encounter, separate design)
- No mechanical rules for the data crystal beyond "free pass for 10 minutes" — the crystal's full significance unfolds in Session 3
- No Taris negotiation skill challenge — her interactions are pure roleplay, not mechanical tests
