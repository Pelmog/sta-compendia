# The Silent Cartographer — Adventure Design Document

**System:** Star Trek Adventures 2nd Edition
**Sessions:** 2 (2.5–3 hours each)
**Players:** 6 (bridge officers aboard the USS Enterprise)
**Captain:** James T. Kirk (GM-controlled NPC)
**Tone:** Mystery/thriller with horror elements
**Created:** 2026-02-27

---

## Premise

The Enterprise investigates the derelict USS *Arcturus* (NCC-1807), a Miranda-class survey vessel lost 11 months ago during a deep-space cartographic mission. The crew discovers a subspace entity — an ancient, vast intelligence called "the Cartographer" — that feeds on neural patterns. It consumed the minds of the Arcturus's 78 crew members, and now it has detected the Enterprise.

## Themes

- **The ethics of first contact with a truly alien intelligence** — the Cartographer doesn't understand it's killing people
- **Horror of the incomprehensible** — a being that exists between dimensions and thinks in geometries
- **The trolley problem in Starfleet blue** — save 430 by destroying 78 preserved patterns, or risk everything for a chance to save everyone
- **Identity and consciousness** — what does it mean to be "preserved" as a neural pattern in an alien's collection?

## Session 1: "Ghost Ship"

### Act 1: The Signal (~30 min)
- Enterprise diverts to investigate automated distress beacon
- Arcturus found drifting in deep space — intact, powered, empty
- Sensor anomalies hint at subspace entity
- Kirk orders boarding party

### Act 2: Boarding the Arcturus (~60–90 min)
**Investigation locations:**
1. **Transporter Room** — communicator badges arranged in perfect circle
2. **Bridge** — PADDs in geometric patterns, final sensor log showing subspace structure
3. **Crew Quarters** — inhuman precision in organization, evidence of neural rewriting
4. **Science Lab** — Vasari's research, the Modified Subspace Scanner (still active)
5. **Cargo Bay 2** — 78 sleeping bags in a spiral, PADDs with single defining words
6. **Escape Pod Bay** — Vasari's final log, escape attempt, forced return

**Personal logs distributed throughout** (4 entries, progressively disturbing):
- Week 2: Excited discovery (Lt. Okafor)
- Week 5: Shared dreams, growing unease (Ens. Torres)
- Week 9: Fear and realization (Vasari)
- Week 11: Final despair (Vasari, from escape pod)

### Act 3: The Revelation (~45 min)
- Entity makes contact: "I HAVE MAPPED 78 OF YOU. YOU ARE BEAUTIFUL. THERE ARE MORE OF YOU NOW. I WOULD LIKE TO CONTINUE."
- Emergency evacuation from Arcturus
- Entity follows the Enterprise
- **Cliffhanger:** Ensign Chen speaks in the entity's voice; lights flicker blue-violet

## Session 2: "The Cartographer's Reply"

### Act 4: Infection (~45 min)
- 48 hours later, situation deteriorating
- 12 crew members with Subspace Contamination
- Ship systems losing data, power fluctuating
- Chen increasingly controlled by entity
- Kirk: "Find me a way to stop this thing — or at least talk to it."
- Entity Manifestation Effects rolled during this act

### Act 5: Understanding the Enemy (~60 min)
**Extended Task: Decode the Cartographer**
- Progress Track: 12 boxes, Difficulty 2, Resistance 1
- 2 breakthroughs (at 6 boxes and 9 boxes)

**Breakthroughs:**
1. **The Entity's Nature & Two Options** (6 boxes) — it's a researcher, not a predator; doesn't understand its mapping kills people. Two approaches emerge: Communication (risky, hopeful) vs. Subspace Shockwave (safe, destructive)
2. **The Key Insight** (9 boxes) — communication requires allowing partial mapping; volunteer needed

### Act 6: The Choice (~45–60 min)

#### Option A: Communication
- Extended Task (Progress Track 10, Difficulty 3, Resistance 2)
- Volunteer allows partial neural mapping with modified Neural Pattern Shielding
- **Success:** Entity withdraws, 78 crew materialize alive, entity learns from mistake
- **Failure:** Volunteer consumed, must resort to Option B or try again (fewer rescued)

#### Option B: Subspace Shockwave
- Extended Task (Progress Track 8, Difficulty 2, Resistance 1)
- Rig Arcturus warp core to overload, generate subspace disruption
- **Success:** Entity driven away, Arcturus destroyed, 78 patterns lost forever
- **Failure:** Shockwave insufficient, entity enraged, must try with Enterprise's core or communicate

## Characters

### Captain James T. Kirk (Major NPC)
- **Attributes:** CTL 10 / DAR 12 / FIT 11 / INS 10 / PRE 12 / REA 9
- **Disciplines:** CMD 5 / CON 3 / ENG 2 / MED 1 / SCI 2 / SEC 4
- **Personal Threat:** 10 (6 base + 4 values)
- **Values:** Risk is Our Business; I Will Not Kill Today; The Needs of the Many; I Am the Captain
- **Focuses:** Inspiration, Persuasion, Hand-to-Hand Combat, Starship Tactics, Xenobiology, Lead by Example
- **Talents:** Bold: Command, Decisive Leadership, Dauntless, Veteran

### USS Arcturus (NCC-1807) — Miranda-class Survey Vessel
- **Scale:** 4
- **Systems:** COM 9 / COMP 9 / ENG 9 / SEN 11 / STR 9 / WEP 9
- **Departments:** CMD 2 / CON 2 / ENG 2 / MED 2 / SCI 4 / SEC 2
- **Talents:** Advanced Sensor Suite, Dedicated Survey Systems, Improved Warp Drive

### Lt. Cmdr. Elena Vasari (Notable NPC)
- Arcturus XO, science specialist; consumed by the entity
- Voice in the personal logs, echo used by entity for communication
- **Personal Threat:** 3
- **Value:** There Is Always More to Discover
- **Focuses:** Subspace Phenomena, Xenolinguistics, Astrophysics

### Ensign David Chen (Minor NPC)
- Enterprise operations officer; first to show entity contamination
- Used in Act 4 horror scenes; entity speaks through him
- Minor NPCs are instantly defeated — no Personal Threat track

### The Cartographer (Entity — no actor sheet)
- Vast subspace intelligence; exists between dimensions
- Attacks: Reason 12 + Science 5 (opposed task, 3 dice)
- Cannot be physically harmed; driven by Threat pool
- Not evil — curious, a researcher that doesn't understand death
