# Plato's Cave — Art Brief for Session 3

## Style Guide

All images should follow the **Subspace Cartography** aesthetic (see `sessions/session03-assets/battlemap-philosophy.md`):
- The Deep Listener's reconstruction style: mathematically precise, slightly wrong, violet geometric signatures bleeding through at edges
- Painterly digital art, cinematic composition, top-down for battlemaps, dramatic angle for handout art
- Colour palette: ice blues, cold whites, warm gold (outpost lighting) contaminated by violet at edges and corners
- The "wrongness" factor: hexagonal ice, too-symmetrical architecture, a sky that's violet instead of grey
