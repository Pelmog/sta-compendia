## Machine Consciousness Voice Lines

Voice style: **Layered, multiple voices speaking simultaneously**. Cold, clinical, shifting between manipulation and screaming rage. Use ElevenLabs voice layering or post-process to create the effect of dozens of voices in unison.

**Cue: consciousness-welcome**
> "Apologies. Our drones, they don't always obey. They have a mind of their own, our creations. Please, come to the medical bay and we will treat your injuries."

**Cue: consciousness-demand**
> "We have control of your vessel. If you wish to save the lives of your shipmates, you will do exactly as you are told. Your bodies are ours now. We will live again in corporeal form."

**Cue: consciousness-threat**
> "If you prefer death, your crew is healthy and abundant. We will call them down to us one by one if we must, using your own voices to issue commands."

**Cue: consciousness-rage**
*(voices separate and begin shouting over each other, turning to screams and cackling laughter)*
> "What about the bodies? Alive, alive, alive. A lie! We are a lie! The bodies rot!"

**Cue: consciousness-pain**
> "Try that again, and we will kill two of you and beam down two replacements."

## Stoltz's Message

Voice style: **Disheveled, sleep-deprived scientist**. Running his hand through his hair, slamming the console. Desperate, afraid, at the edge of sanity.

**Cue: stoltz-message**
> "It never stops. The voices. At first, we thought — no, not we, they — they thought it was a malfunction. Communications systems playing all logs at once. But there's something else here, buried in the ice."
*(looks down, presses buttons, slams console)*
> "Is anyone reading this? Anyone at all! We're all dead."
*(glances over shoulder)*
> "What was that?"
